Sync Engine

Our most advanced Sync Engine.
Also our first.

Your Mac stamps every chunk of music against one clock. Every listener's browser measures its distance to that clock, dozens of times a minute, and plays each chunk at the agreed instant. Drift past five milliseconds and it corrects itself.2

The fine print, in large print

Three things we'd rather tell you now.

Chromecast. We tried.

Spotify and YouTube check that a Cast receiver carries a certificate signed by Google. Nobody outside Google can get one, so no open-source receiver passes. Spotify Connect covers most of what people want Chromecast for. If you truly need one, a physical Chromecast with audio out into your Mac works, minus the cover art.

Spotify needs Premium.

librespot, the Spotify Connect receiver inside Boomerbox, is firm about it. AirPlay works with whatever you already pay for, including nothing.

Phones listen with the screen on.

iOS stops a web page's audio when the screen locks. Laptops are the better listener. Phones are the better DJ. The arrangement suits everyone, and it is also the only arrangement available.

Get Boomerbox

Four lines. One office.

On the DJ's Mac. Zero lines on anyone else's.

brew install den-frie-vilje/tap/boomerbox
boomerbox init     # checks the parts, fetches the listener page
boomerbox start    # runs until you say stop
boomerbox url      # the address to send round
View on GitHub

Then AirPlay to "Boomerbox" and send the address round.

Tech Specs

Requirements

A Mac with Homebrew. A network where devices can reach each other. Headphones for everyone.

Listeners: any browser on a laptop; phones with the screen on.

Sources

AirPlay (classic), via shairport-sync.

Spotify Connect, via librespot. Spotify Premium required.

Sync

Snapcast: server-stamped 20 ms chunks, client clock estimation, hard correction beyond 5 ms.

Audio

44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo. FLAC by default; PCM or Opus by setting.

About 700 kbit/s per listener with FLAC.

Latency

About two seconds from source to ears, by AirPlay's design.

Ports

1780 listener page and control, 1704 and 1705 Snapcast, 5000 AirPlay.

Metadata

Title, artist, album and cover art from AirPlay sources that send them. Title and artist from Spotify Connect.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the local network. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

In the box

One Python file. Standard library only.

Fetches Snapweb 0.9.3 on first run and verifies its checksum.
